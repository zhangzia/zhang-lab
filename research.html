Power System Dynamics & Stability

How does the grid respond to disturbances, and how can we assess and improve its stability?

Nonlinear dynamics provides the foundation for our work on synchronization, stability regions, and online stability assessment. We study how disturbances spread through power networks and how control can improve their response.

Featured project · NSF · 2023–2027

NSF ASCENT: Online stability assessment for inverter-rich grids

ASCENT develops methods for online stability assessment, flexibility, and enhancement of power systems dominated by inverter-based resources, in collaboration with Cornell, Illinois Institute of Technology, Texas Tech, and NYPA.

Project details ↗

Selected papers

2020 · Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena

Fast basin stability estimation for dynamic systems under large perturbations with sequential support vector machine ↗

Uses a sequential support vector machine to focus expensive simulations near the boundary between stable and unstable behavior, making stability estimation under large disturbances more efficient.

2019 · IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics

A Basin Stability Based Metric for Ranking the Transient Stability of Generators ↗

Develops a simulation-based measure for ranking generators by their resilience to disturbances and examines how loading, line reactance, and machine settings affect that ranking.

Inverter-Based Resource Integration

How can renewable generation and battery storage connect to and support the grid?

We model and control inverter-based resources, including renewables and battery storage. Our work connects grid-forming controls, hybrid plants, and system integration, with DOE ADAPT and RESPECT bringing these questions into plant control and storage demonstrations.

Featured project · DOE · 2021–2027

DOE ADAPT: Controls for hybrid PV plants

ADAPT develops asynchronous, distributed, and adaptive parameter tuning for hybrid photovoltaic plants, coordinating solar generation, storage, and inverter controls to support grid performance.

Project details ↗

Selected papers

2025 · 2025 IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM)

Transient Stability Enhancement via Grid-Forming IBRs on a 5000-bus New York Transmission System ↗

Evaluates how grid-forming inverter controls, fault locations, and pre-fault power flows affect transient stability in a 5,000-bus New York State transmission model.

2024 · 2024 IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM)

Transient Stability of Transmission and Distribution Grids with Grid-Forming Inverter-Based Resources: Phasor Domain Modeling ↗

Develops phasor-domain models of droop, virtual synchronous machine, and virtual oscillator controls, together with transmission–distribution co-simulation, to study grid-forming resource integration and post-fault stability.

Power System Operations & Economics

How should grid resources be scheduled, operated and valued under uncertainty?

We connect physical system operation with economic decisions: dispatch, storage scheduling, hybrid-plant bidding, and electricity prices. Optimization and forecasting help evaluate the value of flexible resources under uncertainty.

Featured project · DOE · 2026–2029 · $5 million

DOE RESPECT: Long-duration storage for critical facilities

Resilient Energy System based on Polymer Electrolyte and Ceramic Technology (RESPECT) develops a planned 100 kW / 1.2 MWh storage demonstration at Binghamton’s ES2 data-center test environment, addressing backup power, resilience, and peak-demand management.

Principal investigator: Ziang Zhang · Co-investigator: Zixiao Ma. Partners: Electrovaya USA, LiiON, Eaton, and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory.

Project details ↗

Selected papers

2025 · Applied Energy

Advancing energy system optimization via data-centric task-oriented forecasting: An application in PV-battery operation ↗

Develops data-centric task-oriented forecasting frameworks that improve downstream PV-battery day-ahead market operation by iteratively refining training data.

2022 · 2022 IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM)

Arbitrage and Capacity Firming in Coordination with Day-Ahead Bidding of a Hybrid PV Plant ↗

Coordinates hybrid-PV day-ahead bidding with battery arbitrage and capacity firming through two-stage robust optimization that accounts for renewable uncertainty and under-generation penalties.
