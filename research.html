Selected research projects

DOE · 2026–2029 · $5 million

RESPECT: Long-duration storage for critical facilities

Resilient Energy System based on Polymer Electrolyte and Ceramic Technology (RESPECT) develops a planned 100 kW / 1.2 MWh storage demonstration at Binghamton’s ES2 data-center test environment. The project addresses backup power, resilience, and peak-demand management.

Principal investigator: Ziang Zhang · Co-investigator: Zixiao Ma
Project partners: Electrovaya USA, LiiON, Eaton, and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory.
