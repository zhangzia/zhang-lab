Model, assumptions, and sources

A classical synchronous generator connected to an ideal infinite bus. All powers share the machine MVA base. Electrical frequency is 60 Hz; pre-fault Pmax = 2 pu. The fault starts at t = 0 and forces electrical power to zero. Clearing switches instantaneously to the selected post-fault network.

dδ/dt = Δω
dΔω/dt = (2πf / 2H) [Pₘ − Pmax sin(δ)]

δ is electrical radians; Δω is the electrical angular-speed deviation in rad/s. Pmax = 0 during the fault. Initial conditions are δ₀ = asin(Pₘ / 2), Δω₀ = 0. The equal-area boundary gives the critical clearing angle; constant fault-on acceleration then gives the critical clearing time. Post-fault trajectories use fourth-order Runge–Kutta with a 1 ms step and an 8 s horizon. The chart ends early if the angle exceeds one full revolution.

Stable means a bounded first swing in this ideal undamped model; oscillations do not decay. This tool does not model excitation, governors, inverter controls, storage dynamics, or a real multi-machine network. A weaker post-fault network can be unstable even with immediate clearing.
