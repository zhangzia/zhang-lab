Reading LCOS

How you use storage changes its cost.

Duration, cycling frequency, efficiency, charging prices and financing affect the cost per MWh delivered. The published cases model different applications. The chart does not estimate project revenue or the value of backup power.

Explore the cost of generating electricity (LCOE)

Data & coverage

The explorer uses selected $/MWh ranges from Lazard’s annual LCOS analyses and combined LCOE+ reports. The chart groups recurring applications, with the reported size and duration beneath each case. The data table and CSV also include less common cases from early reports, such as island and microgrid systems.

Case definitions, charging assumptions and methods change over time. The 100 MW, four-hour benchmark uses the case Lazard tracks from 2020 onward. Reported dollars are shown without an inflation adjustment. Years and cases without source data are never estimated.
