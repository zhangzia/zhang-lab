NYPA education & workforce training

Clean Energy Workforce Pipeline

From the classroom
to the electric grid.

Binghamton University’s NYPA-funded program connects power systems education with industry practice through courses, simulation labs, guest seminars, and paid internship opportunities.

Led by Professor Ziang “John” Zhang, the program brings together the New York Power Authority (NYPA), NYSEG / AVANGRID, and the New York Independent System Operator (NYISO).

Fundamentals of Power Systems

EECE 443 connects grid history, power system analysis, renewable energy, storage, markets, policy, and power-industry careers. Guest speakers and industry activities are built into the course.

02

NSF Energy Storage Engine training

Building the energy-storage workforce

Our training activities contribute to the NSF Energy Storage Engine in Upstate New York, led by Binghamton University. The Engine connects education, workforce pathways, and regional energy-storage innovation.

Battery education also includes faculty participation in the New Energy New York Battery Academy, with learning opportunities spanning battery fundamentals, safety, and management systems.
